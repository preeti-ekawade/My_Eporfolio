    MICROWAVESARLiDARHYPERSPECTRAL

    This subject helped me understand advanced remote sensing techniques, including microwave remote sensing, SAR, LiDAR, and hyperspectral imaging. I learned how radar signals interact with different surface features and how LiDAR and hyperspectral data are used to study vegetation, terrain, and environmental conditions. The introduction to SAR data processing using ESA SNAP helped me connect theoretical concepts with practical applications. This subject improved my understanding of satellite data and its role in agricultural and environmental monitoring.
